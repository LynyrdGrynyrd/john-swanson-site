Privacy policy

Gitea Private Backups uses Google Drive authorization only to store and verify the account owner's encrypted backups.

Data accessed and stored

The application uses the drive.file permission to create its backup folder, upload encrypted archives, inspect their file metadata and download them for verification or recovery. It can access files created by or explicitly authorized for this app; it does not request general access to the account's other Drive files.

Google receives encrypted archive bytes and associated file metadata, such as filenames, sizes and timestamps. Archive contents are encrypted before upload. The recovery passphrase is kept separately and is not uploaded with the backups.

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in protected files on owner-controlled computers used to administer or run the backup. Operational receipts record backup identifiers, timestamps, byte counts, hashes and outcomes. They do not intentionally include OAuth tokens or recovery passphrases.

Use and sharing

Data is used for the owner's backup, integrity verification and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising, supplied for AI model training or shared with other users. Google processes the files as the storage provider. Hosting providers may process ordinary request metadata to serve these information pages; the pages contain no analytics, third-party scripts or forms.

Retention and control

Backups are retained until the owner removes them. This configuration does not automatically prune or delete Drive archives. The owner can stop future access by revoking the application in Google Account connections. Revocation does not delete existing archives or local copies; those must be managed separately by the owner.
