Terms of use

This is an owner-operated personal backup utility. It is not offered as a public backup or hosting service.

Authorized use

The operator may use the tool only with accounts and data they own or are authorized to administer. The tool's Google Drive access depends on the account owner's consent. Google Drive remains subject to Google's own terms and storage limits.

Backup responsibility

The owner is responsible for retaining the recovery passphrase separately, checking backup results and testing restoration. A successful upload alone does not establish recoverability. No guarantee of uninterrupted operation or successful recovery is made.

Stopping use

The owner can stop the scheduled backup and revoke the app's Google authorization. Existing backup files remain until removed separately. There is no subscription, charge or public user registration for this utility.

For questions, contact John Swanson. The privacy policy describes how backup-related data is handled.
